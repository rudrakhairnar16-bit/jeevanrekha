# JeevanRekha — Final Jury Cut

This landing is intentionally a short scroll-controlled film, not the full product manual.

## Locked narrative
Human life → non-graphic emergency → bystander → Tier 0 → verified responder/Tier 1 → factual family alert → routine care reset → identity ≠ permission → federated access → sealed relationships → family capabilities → privacy-preserving population signal → transparency → digital failure → CARE CONTINUES → finale.

## Pacing
- 16 compact beats, roughly half a viewport of scroll per weighted unit.
- Desktop prelude shortened so the story begins quickly.
- Technical explanations remain collapsed or route to `/prototype` and `/architecture`.
- Native scrolling, deterministic progress, reverse-scroll semantics and reduced-motion/static fallback remain intact.

## Product truth
CARE IS NEVER DENIED BECAUSE JEEVANREKHA FAILED. JeevanRekha is a fictional concept/prototype, not a Government of India service. Records remain federated; identity is not permission; family is not automatic consent; emergency access is scoped and audited; population views exclude identity.
